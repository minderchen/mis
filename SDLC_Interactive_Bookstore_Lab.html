Your business case

Campus Bookstore

THE PROBLEM · AS-IS

Students call or visit to check textbook availability. Staff maintain inventory in spreadsheets.

THE SOLUTION · TO-BE

Students reserve books online while a shared inventory system keeps stock accurate.

SUCCESS MEANS

Accurate stock, reliable reservations, shorter wait times, and better student experience.

About this teaching model

These values are hypothetical—not estimates for a real bookstore. Assumptions: each planned feature requires 3 team-weeks plus 12 team-weeks of shared setup/integration work; the team can deliver up to 3 team-weeks of work per calendar week; fixed cost is $8,000 plus $2,000 per team-week. Available capacity is the smaller of schedule capacity and budget capacity. A shortfall signals delivery pressure and potential quality risk; it is not a forecast of actual software quality. Real projects also have dependencies, risks, skill constraints, and variation in productivity.
